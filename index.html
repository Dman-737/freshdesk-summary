Methodology

All 267 tickets were pulled directly from the FreshDesk All Tickets view (every status, all-time). Each ticket's ID, subject, contact, type, priority, and status were captured. Tickets were then auto-classified into ExpoGenie modules using keyword pattern-matching on the subject line; clusters were identified by repeating phrases. The classification is heuristic — a small number of tickets fall into the "General Question / Misc" bucket because the subject didn't contain a recognizable feature keyword. The full raw data, including every ticket and its classification, is in the companion Excel workbook.
